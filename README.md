# HPCL Visakh Refinery – Residue Upgradation Technologies

## Industrial Internship

**Organization:** Hindustan Petroleum Corporation Limited (HPCL)  
**Location:** Visakh Refinery, Visakhapatnam, India  
**Department:** Technical – Process Analysis and Design  
**Duration:** May 2026 – July 2026  
**Area:** Residue Upgradation Technologies

---

## Overview

This repository documents my industrial internship at Hindustan Petroleum
Corporation Limited (HPCL), Visakh Refinery, focused on residue upgradation
technologies used to convert heavy refinery residues into higher-value
products.

The internship involved studying refinery process units, residue
characterization, residue conversion technologies, hydrocracking, process
calculations, refinery integration, and industrial safety.

---

## Key Technical Areas

- Residue Upgradation Technologies
- Residue Hydrocracking
- LC-MAX™ Ebullated-Bed Technology
- Delayed Coking
- Residue Fluid Catalytic Cracking (RFCC)
- Solvent Deasphalting (SDA)
- Residue Characterization
- API Gravity and Conradson Carbon Residue (CCR)
- SARA Analysis
- Hydrogen Consumption
- Sulphur Mass Balance
- Refinery Process Integration
- Industrial Safety

---

## Plant Exposure

During the internship, I gained technical exposure to:

- Crude Distillation Unit (CDU)
- Vacuum Distillation Unit (VDU)
- Full Conversion Hydrocracker Unit (FCHCU)
- Residue Upgradation Facility (RUF)

---

## Work Performed

- Studied HPCL's Residue Upgradation Facility (RUF) and LC-MAX™
  hydrocracking technology.
- Compared major residue conversion routes including delayed coking,
  RFCC, and solvent deasphalting.
- Analyzed residue properties including API gravity, CCR, SARA fractions,
  sulphur, and metals.
- Performed representative calculations involving residue conversion,
  hydrogen consumption, CCR blending, and sulphur balance.
- Studied refinery integration, process flow schemes, and safety
  considerations associated with high-pressure hydroprocessing.
